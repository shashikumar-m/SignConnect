Instructions

  1. Type the word/sign name in the label box
  2. Position your hand clearly in front of camera
  3. Click Start Recording
  4. Wait for countdown, then do the sign naturally
  5. Repeat 30-60 times per sign for best accuracy
  6. Use Export to save your data
  7. Run: python ml-server/train_lstm.py
Tip: For body signs (Mother, Father, Doctor), step back so your upper body is visible. For hand signs (A-Z), keep hand close to camera.
